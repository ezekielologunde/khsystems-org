# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
- Maryland Medicaid members (and the parents or guardians of children and teens) looking for outpatient mental health care or a psychiatric rehabilitation program (PRP). Often on a phone, often stressed, sometimes in a hurry. Many are Black and Baltimore-area families.
- Referring clinicians, case managers, and school or hospital social workers who need to confirm fit (Medicaid only, locations, programs) and refer quickly.
- Job seekers (clinicians) arriving from Indeed or LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose
Public website for King Health Systems, Inc., a licensed Adult and Child Outpatient Mental Health Clinic and PRP (Maryland Department of Health OHCQ), CARF-accredited. Goal: a visitor knows within seconds that this is a Medicaid-accepting clinic near them, and can call or request an appointment with one tap. Secondary: build trust (real clinicians, accreditation) and bring in search traffic through the Learn section (articles, news, PHQ-9 and GAD-7 check-ins).

Verified facts only: accepts Maryland Medicaid only; phone 410-578-4340; offices in Baltimore, Laurel, and Cockeysville; Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM; in-office, at-home, and telehealth care.

## Brand Personality
Warm, steady, plain-spoken. A trusted neighbor who is also a licensed professional. Dignified, never clinical-cold, never cutesy.

## Anti-references
- Stock "sad person with head in hands" mental-health imagery.
- Pastel blob gradients, glass panels, doodles and stickers as decoration.
- Hero-metric templates and icon-card grids as page structure.
- Anything that implies a guarantee, a cure, or a testimonial we cannot show.

## Design Principles
1. The first viewport answers: who is this for, can I use my insurance, where are you, how do I reach you now.
2. Real people and real places beat illustration. Photos carry the warmth, so the interface can stay quiet.
3. One obvious next step per screen (call, or request an appointment), repeated, never competing.
4. Crisis safety is always one tap away (988 / 911 on the Learn pages).
5. Honest claims only. If we cannot verify it, we do not print it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, 44px touch targets, visible keyboard focus, meaningful alt text on every photo, reduced-motion respected, English and Spanish.
